# Weibull fitting

Guidance for reviewing PARALLAX multi-agent reliability investigations on its synthetic demonstration dataset.

Record event versus right-censored counts and the chosen time unit. Verify fitted shape and scale are positive, and compare estimated survival to observed cohort behavior. A computed B10 life is model-dependent, not a guarantee.

## Reviewer record
- Dataset version and commit SHA
- Selected tool and its input arguments
- Evidence, caveats, and unresolved questions
- Reviewer decision and follow-up
