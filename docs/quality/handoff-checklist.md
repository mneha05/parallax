# Handoff checklist

Guidance for reviewing PARALLAX multi-agent reliability investigations on its synthetic demonstration dataset.

Before showing a stakeholder brief, confirm synthetic versus real origin, units, sample coverage, censoring, reproducibility notes, and known alternative explanations. Keep human approval between model recommendations and operational decisions.

## Reviewer record
- Dataset version and commit SHA
- Selected tool and its input arguments
- Evidence, caveats, and unresolved questions
- Reviewer decision and follow-up
