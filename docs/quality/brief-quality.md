# Brief quality rubric

Guidance for reviewing PARALLAX multi-agent reliability investigations on its synthetic demonstration dataset.

A useful brief contains a precise headline, evidence with denominators and uncertainty, feasible recommended actions, and caveats. Reject unsupported causal language and recommendations that exceed the quality of the observed data.

## Reviewer record
- Dataset version and commit SHA
- Selected tool and its input arguments
- Evidence, caveats, and unresolved questions
- Reviewer decision and follow-up
